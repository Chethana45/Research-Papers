## 📌 About the Research

**FlightLab** is a research-oriented project focused on exploring aircraft flight fundamentals through computational modeling, interactive simulation, and visualization.

The research connects fundamental flight principles with computer-based simulation to make complex aerodynamic concepts easier to understand, analyze, and experiment with.

It explores how computational techniques can represent aircraft behavior under different flight conditions and provide an interactive environment for studying flight dynamics.

## 🎯 Research Objectives

- Explore the fundamental principles of aircraft flight.
- Study the aerodynamic forces acting on an aircraft.
- Understand the relationship between lift, drag, thrust, and weight.
- Investigate aircraft behavior under varying flight conditions.
- Explore computational approaches for visualizing aircraft performance.
- Bridge theoretical aerospace concepts with interactive software-based learning.

## 🔬 Research Areas

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| Aerodynamics | Lift, drag, and aerodynamic forces |
| Flight Mechanics | Aircraft motion and flight behavior |
| Aircraft Stability | Longitudinal, lateral, and directional stability |
| Flight Simulation | Computational modeling and visualization |
| Aircraft Performance | Effects of velocity, altitude, and weight |

## 💡 Research Motivation

As a Computer Science student with a strong interest in the fundamentals of flight, I wanted to explore how software engineering and computational techniques could be used to understand aircraft behavior.

FlightLab represents an effort to connect two different domains — Computer Science and Aerospace Engineering — through research, simulation, and interactive visualization.
